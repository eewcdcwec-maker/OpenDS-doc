# Organization and team

## Joining

- **Register**: when the platform allows self-registration, create an account and an
  organization on the sign-up page and accept the participation terms of the dataspace. A
  platform administrator approves the organization before it can exchange data. If
  registration is closed, ask to be invited.
- **Be invited**: an owner or administrator adds you as a member, and the invitation email
  contains a code that you enter when accepting. Without an email server, the inviter is shown
  the code once and passes it on. If an account with your email address already exists, it
  joins the organization with its own name and password.

A new account receives an email with a link to choose a password, which is valid for seven
days. Only the most recent link works, so requesting a password reset replaces an unused
invitation link. If the platform has no email server, password resets are not available and
inviters set the initial password themselves.

Until your organization is approved, you can sign in and look around but cannot exchange data,
and a notice at the top shows whether the organization is awaiting approval, rejected or
suspended.

You can decline an invitation or leave an organization at any time, unless you are its last
owner, in which case you appoint another owner first. An owner or administrator can also remove
a member. In both cases the account itself remains, together with its other organizations.

## Your profile

Open the menu under your name and choose **Edit profile** to change your name or password. A
new password needs at least 16 characters, and changing it signs out every session of your
account, including the current one. After five wrong passwords in a row, the account is locked
for ten minutes.

## Organization details

Under **Organization** you describe what your organization shares or uses in the dataspace, and
partners see this description. Signed-in members can also see the other approved organizations
of their dataspace and their running connectors in the organization directory, but never any
user data.

Owners and administrators also manage:

- **Members**: the members and their roles, on the same page.
- **Organization settings**: access requests and activity tracking.
- **Connector access**: Manager or Viewer rights for a member, granted and revoked on the
  connector's page.
- **Partner groups** and the **Organization activity** log, each on its own page in the
  sidebar.

The list of usage purposes is defined by the platform administrator for the whole dataspace,
and providers choose from it in their usage policies.

## API keys

Every member can create a personal API key for scripts under **API access**. Creating a key
asks for your password and an expiry of 30 days, 90 days, one year or never, and the key is
shown only once. A key acts as you, with your roles. **Replace key** makes the old key stop
working, while **Revoke key** removes it. How to use the key is described under
[API](/user-guide/api).
